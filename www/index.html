About

Video streamed as ANSI art over Telnet, served frame-by-frame at the original framerate. Open a terminal and connect to get started.

Requirements

  • ANSI / VT100 escape sequence support
  • UTF-8 character encoding
  • Terminal size: at least {{COLS}} × {{ROWS}} columns / rows

⚠ Windows users: use PuTTY or WSL — the built-in Windows Telnet client defaults to CP437 and will display garbage.
